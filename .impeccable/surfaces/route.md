# Surface brief — / (single-page index)

Scope: the whole single-page app (global reach view + creator lookup + methods note).
Visitor mode: Operate. The visitor reads evidence and completes one task (creator lookup).
Audience: Hugging Face creators, model evaluators, Heretic community members checking adoption.
Job: grasp the current scale and velocity of public Heretic models within seconds; optionally rank one creator's models.
Action/task: read the live figure, run a creator lookup, follow out to Hugging Face.
Proof/content: aggregate download intelligence (all-time, 30-day, model count), ranked creator rows, tag-plus-name methodology.
Constraints: all factual copy, methodology, lookup flow, ticker math, degraded/loading/empty states, and accessibility behavior stay intact — reskin and restructure only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A data-desk broadsheet that inks itself. The page refuses the dark dashboard-metric template the whole category ships: instead of stat cards under an eyebrow, one black figure plate carries the cumulative total as a just-printed numeral with a red live trace inking its pace beneath it, on warm paper with hairline rules. Scale awe comes from print authority plus visible motion, not from neon.

OWN-WORLD: Warm newsprint paper ground, ink near-black, one signal-red live ink; hairline rules, folio masthead with dateline, figure captions, ledger rows with leader rules, stamped status box. Spectral (serif) speaks the editorial argument; Archivo (grotesque) sets the masthead, labels, and figures; system mono reads raw measurements only. No cards-as-structure, no eyebrow kicker, no gradient/glass/chrome.

STORY: The visitor understands in seconds that Heretic's public footprint is large and still moving (ticking numeral + inking trace), believes it because the method is printed beside the numbers like a figure note, and acts by looking up a creator in the boxed inquiry form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Folio masthead (nameplate, dateline, stamped LIVE/DEGRADED status, updated line). Standfirst lede. Fig. 1 black plate: caption, giant paper numeral ticking live, red trace canvas inking the pace with minute ticks, pace annotation. Ledger rows for 30-day volume and model count. Boxed creator-inquiry form. Primary action: the Inspect control in the inquiry box.

FORM: Assigned direction 6 of 7 grounded candidates (seismograph strip-chart translated into pinned broadsheet materials), seed key 7ed557ff. Raises carried: rain-garden (trace leaves persistent ink history), miura (creator rows deploy staged), hypercard (every model row anchor-addressable), parametric (trace ink weight follows the rate), catalog-wall (focused row lifts, others dim).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
